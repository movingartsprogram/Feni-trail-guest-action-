Chapter I

The farm

Dudhsagar Plantation is an organic farm stay in the forest belt of South Goa. Cashew, spice and coconut plantations, waterfalls tucked into the green, and a copper still at the centre of it. It won Gold for Sustainable Leadership at the 2025 Outlook Traveler Responsible Tourism Awards.

It is a working farm, not a resort. Expect red earth on your shoes, and a night that is quieter than you are used to.

Chapter III

What to bring

Rough farm stay. Dress for dirt, dew and a waterfall. Tick as you pack; the ticks stay on your phone.

Do

  • Arrive in daylight, with time to spare.
  • Eat with every drink. Food and water will be there all weekend.
  • Ask questions. Everyone here has a story.
  • Sit close for Elvis, phone away until the set ends.

Don't

  • Bring your own bottles.
  • Swim after drinking, or if you are not a confident swimmer.
  • Wander off the paths alone after dark.

You are driving yourself. Saturday's drive is the one to plan. For Sunday's, agree with whoever you are travelling with who is driving home, and leave when you are properly clear.
