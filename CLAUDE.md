# Feni Trail guest page

The "Before you come" page for guests of The Feni Trail, 17–18 October 2026,
Dudhsagar Plantation. Live at https://movingartsprogram.github.io/Feni-trail-guest-action-/
(GitHub Pages, served from `main`).

## Ground rules

- `index.html` on `main` is the latest version. If someone uploads an
  `index.html`, it may be older: compare it with `main` and merge changes in.
  Never replace the repo's file with the upload.
- Do not bring back anything listed under "Decided and done" below.
- Everything is in one file, `index.html`. No build step.

## Decided and done (do not undo)

- Design: vintage type (EB Garamond, Playfair Display, IM Fell English SC),
  "Chapter I–V" eyebrows, alternating dark green / paper sections, farm
  sounds button.
- Section order: Farm → Tell us about you → What to bring → Saturday plan →
  Your hosts → Contribution → sign-off.
- Photography removed from the "What do you want to do?" options.
- "Photograph the hosts without asking" removed from the Don't list.
- Contribution: ₹3,000 per guest, paid via https://razorpay.me/@movingartsprogram
- Sign-off: "One last thing. This is going to be rough..."
- Couples fill in one form: "Are you coming with a partner?" opens a partner
  section with their own food, allergies, spice, drinks, swimming and injury.
  Activities, emergency contact and "anything else" are asked once.
- "Clear my answers" button. Answers are kept on the guest's phone
  (localStorage) until they clear them, on purpose.
- Answers go to Prashant on WhatsApp (919849690077). Nothing is stored on a
  server.
